Valuation of Financial Service Firms

Valuation

Financial service firms are often associated with ambiguous capital structure and the difficulty in pinpointing reinvestment, making traditional DCF analysis particularly challenging to apply. So we should approach the valuation of such firms with equity-focused measures and modified reinvestment definitions, considering the following key approaches:

  • Use dividend discount model (DDM) – Apply DDM as the primary valuation framework, ensuring stock repurchases are incorporated into the dividend component to capture the full picture of cash returned to equity holders
  • Redefine reinvestment for DCF – Use the cost incurred to fulfill regulatory capital requirement ratios as a proxy for reinvestment, replacing the traditional capital expenditure and working capital measures that are difficult to isolate within financial service firms
  • Use equity measures instead of total capital – Replace firm-level total capital measures with equity-level measures in DCF, focusing on equity value directly rather than attempting to separate debt from equity in an inherently ambiguous capital structure
