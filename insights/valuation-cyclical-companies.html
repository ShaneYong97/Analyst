Valuation of Cyclical Companies

Valuation

Cyclical companies are often associated with earnings that fluctuate significantly across the business cycle, causing noise and inconsistency in valuation multiples. So we should approach the valuation of such companies with normalised earnings that smooth out cyclical distortions, considering the following key approaches:

  • Normalise using historical earnings – Average earnings over a sufficiently long historical period, ensuring the data spans a complete business cycle to avoid anchoring to peak or trough conditions
  • Normalise using average margins – Apply a normalised operating margin (e.g. sector or company average over the cycle) to current or mid-cycle revenue to derive a cleaned earnings estimate, reducing the noise from transitory cost or demand swings
  • Use sector averages for data-limited companies – Where a company lacks sufficient historical data to cover a full business cycle, substitute with sector-average margins or multiples as a proxy for normalised profitability
